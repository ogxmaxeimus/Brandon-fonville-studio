Reznik Law website pitch

Use this if they reply to your email or you get them on a short call. Keep it conversational. Do not read it like a telemarketer.

1. Open (15 seconds)

You say

“Hi [Name], this is Brandon Fonville. I sent a quick note about your website. Do you have two minutes, or is there a better time later this week?”

If busy: “No problem. I’ll keep it to email. Would tomorrow afternoon work for a short follow-up?”

2. Why you called (20 seconds)

“I was reviewing local estate planning firms and looked at carywills.com. Your credentials are strong. Board certification, clear niche. The site doesn’t quite match that level of trust yet, so I built a concept redesign to show what I mean.”

3. What’s off on the live site (30–45 seconds)

Pick 3. Don’t dump the whole list.

  • It still feels like a DIY Wix build, not a specialist firm site.
  • Long text blocks make it hard to scan what you do and how to start.
  • The first impression doesn’t reflect board-certified estate planning work.
  • Key pages exist, but the path to schedule is easier to miss than it should be.
  • Families deciding who to trust need calm clarity fast. The current layout makes them work for it.
“None of that means the practice is weak. It means the website is underselling the practice.”

4. What you made (20 seconds)

“I put together a multi-page concept using your logo and brand blue. Same firm, clearer structure, calmer homepage, and a cleaner path to schedule. Happy to walk you through it in five minutes, or I can send the link again.”

5. The ask

“Would you be open to a short screen share this week so I can show the concept? If it’s useful, we can talk about a redesign quote. If not, no pressure.”

If yes

Book a time. Send the concept link before the call.

If maybe

“I’ll email the concept and three specific notes on the current site. You can glance when you have a minute.”

If no

“Understood. Appreciate your time. If the site comes up later, I’m easy to find at brandonfonville.com.”

6. If they ask about price

“For a firm site like this, redesigns usually land in a project range once we confirm pages and content. I’m happy to price it after a quick walkthrough so the number matches what you actually need.”

Optional range if pressed: homepage + core pages often falls around a few thousand depending on scope; monthly care is separate.

Don’t

  • Don’t insult Wix or their taste.
  • Don’t pitch Afterlight or other products on this call.
  • Don’t send a long feature dump. Show the concept.
  • Don’t chase if they say no cleanly.
