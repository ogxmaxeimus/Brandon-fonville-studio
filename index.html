About the studio

Brandon Fonville Creative Studio operates like a small professional design agency, not just a one off freelancer. Every engagement runs through discovery, proposal, contract, structured revisions, and production ready handoff.

My work focuses on turning ideas into clean, functional, and visually impactful designs that help businesses and creators bring their concepts to life in a way that feels modern, intentional, and professional.

Beyond the visuals, I handle project coordination, contracts, revisions, production prep, and print setup, delivering complete design solutions that are ready for execution.

The studio process

Every project runs through a professional workflow: discovery, contract, design, and delivery.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Client completes the discovery questionnaire covering goals, audience, budget, and timeline.

  2. 02

    Strategy call

    We align on the problem, solution, scope, and what success looks like before design begins.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    You receive a clear scope, timeline, investment, and next steps, with no guesswork.

  4. 04

    Contract + deposit

    Signed agreement and deposit (typically 50%) before work starts. Your studio contract library covers every stage.

  5. 05

    Design

    Concepts, structured revisions, and refinement through agreed milestones until it's right.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Final files released after final payment, formatted, consistent, and production ready.

Backed by real studio operations

Proposals, creative briefs, contracts, license agreements, change orders, handoff checklists, and portfolio permissions, managed through the studio's contract library and client agreement system.
