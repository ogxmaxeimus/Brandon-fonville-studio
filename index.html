A-005 · The Method

Stage 01 / 06

Discovery through delivery — drawn before work begins.

  1. 01 — Discovery

    Client completes the discovery questionnaire covering goals, audience, budget, and timeline.

    Week 0—1

  2. 02 — Strategy call

    We align on the problem, solution, scope, and what success looks like before design begins.

    Week 1

  3. 03 — Proposal

    You receive a clear scope, timeline, investment, and next steps — with no guesswork.

    Week 1—2

  4. 04 — Contract + deposit

    Signed agreement and deposit (typically 50%) before work starts.

    Week 2

  5. 05 — Design

    Concepts, structured revisions, and refinement through agreed milestones until it's right.

    Weeks 2—N

  6. 06 — Delivery

    Final files released after final payment — formatted, consistent, and production ready.

    Handoff

Backed by real studio operations

Proposals, creative briefs, contracts, license agreements, change orders, handoff checklists, and portfolio permissions — managed through the studio's contract library and client agreement system.

A-009 · The Atelier

About the studio

Brandon Fonville Creative Studio operates like a small professional design agency — not a one-off freelancer. Every engagement runs through discovery, proposal, contract, structured revisions, and production-ready handoff.

The work turns ideas into clean, functional, and visually impactful designs that help businesses and creators bring concepts to life in a way that feels modern, intentional, and professional.

Beyond the visuals: project coordination, contracts, revisions, production prep, and print setup — complete solutions ready for execution.
