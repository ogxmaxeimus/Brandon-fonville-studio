Services

Estate planning documents that fit real life

Wills, powers of attorney, healthcare directives, and related planning for North Carolina families.

Estate planning is how you organize assets and decisions so your wishes are clear if you become ill or pass away. The firm drafts the documents and explains how they work together.

  1. 01
    Last will and testamentNames beneficiaries, an executor, and guardians for minor children when needed.
  2. 02
    Powers of attorneyLets a trusted person manage finances or healthcare if you cannot.
  3. 03
    Healthcare directivesRecords medical preferences so family members are not guessing under stress.
  4. 04
    College bound planningAdult children often need their own POA and healthcare documents once they turn 18.
