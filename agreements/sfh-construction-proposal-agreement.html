1. The challenge

SFH Construction needs a professional online presence that wins trust with Triangle area homeowners and commercial clients. The current free Wix site understates the business, lacks a custom domain, and is not positioned to convert local leads.

2. Proposed solution

Designer will design and develop a custom marketing website for SFH Construction, launched on sfhconstruction.com, replacing reliance on the Wix placeholder. The site will present services, build trust, and make it easy to request a quote or call.

3. Process

  1. Discovery & intake: brand assets, photos, copy, goals, and domain access.
  2. Build: custom pages, mobile layout, form, and basic SEO.
  3. Review: Client feedback within included revision rounds.
  4. Launch: point domain, go live, and archive Wix as needed.

4. Deliverables

  • Custom website (design and development), not a Wix rebuild
  • Pages: Home, About, Services, Contact / quote request
  • Mobile responsive layout using Client provided logo and brand direction
  • Contact / quote form wired to Client email
  • Basic on page SEO (titles, meta descriptions, Triangle local keywords)
  • Domain launch assistance for sfhconstruction.com
  • 14 days of post launch bug fixes

5. Investment

  1. Total project fee: $2,200.00 USD
  2. Deposit: 50% ($1,100.00) due upon acceptance, before work begins
  3. Balance: 50% ($1,100.00) due before domain go live / final release
  4. Domain registration for sfhconstruction.com (approx. $10 to $15 per year) is separate and reimbursable if Designer purchases on Client’s behalf
  5. Work outside this scope requires a written Change Order

6. Acceptance

By signing below, Client accepts this proposal and the Statement of Work & Terms on page 2. Upon acceptance, Designer will invoice the deposit and schedule kickoff. This proposal is valid for 14 days from August 8, 2026.
