Objections

Three questions attorneys ask first.

Is this a will or estate plan?

No. Afterlight does not replace estate documents or legal counsel. It holds the digital and human instructions your drafting rarely captures - and you stay the authority on the legal side.

What about liability and passwords?

The product is built to store instructions and locations, not master passwords. Clients point stewards to a password manager or paper packet. Your packet can say that plainly on page one.

Why would a client finish this?

Because you introduce it at the moment they already care - after signing. The free local vault proves the flow; Firm Seat gives you branded invites, staff checklist, and PDF cover with your name on it.
